## Identity

- **Full name:** Monash Nexus for Emerging Technologies (MNET)
- **What it is:** A student team under the **Monash Student Team Initiative (MSTI)**, affiliated
  with the Faculty of Information Technology (and increasingly Engineering). Everything MNET
  does falls under the **simulation technologies** umbrella — XR / immersive media, digital
  twins, quantum computing — while staying deliberately broad: robotics and other up-and-coming
  technologies are "not out of bounds" (draft constitution). "Emerging" is read as emerging
  **from the digital realm into the physical** — hence the tagline.
- **Tagline (primary):** **"Enter the Digital Frontier"** · supporting lines: *Access, Upskill,
  Engage* (prospectus) · "Expanding all horizons." · "JOIN US, BUILD THE FUTURE"
- **Home:** The **Digital Makerspace** at Monash Clayton. Team postal address: Building 63,
  25 Exhibition Walk, Clayton VIC 3168.
- **Website:** https://monashemerging.tech · **Repo:** github.com/Monash-Emerging-Tech/web-nexus
- **Reach:** ~8,000+ combined online student following (2025 prospectus figure).

## Mission (canonical copy)

From the 2025 prospectus — the most polished statement of what MNET is; prefer this voice:

> "We are the Monash Nexus for Emerging Technologies. We are a student team focused on exposing
> students to the possibilities offered by XR, Quantum Computing, and more. As a 'nexus', we
> offer passionate and talented students the opportunity to collaborate with research labs, work
> in an industry environment, and get their hands on otherwise inaccessible technology.
> Together, we hope to inspire the next generation of innovators."

Canonical umbrella (Rohan Kalanje, founder — confirmed 2026-07-10): **everything MNET does
falls under simulation technologies.** The "emerging" in the name is the bridge — technologies
emerging from the digital realm into the physical — which is exactly the prospectus's "bringing
the real into the digital and the digital into the real", and why the tagline is **"Enter the
Digital Frontier"**.

Impact framing (prospectus): MNET strives to explore and harness emerging technologies to
reshape **"what can't be" into "what will be"** — "bringing the real into the digital and the
digital into the real". MNET serves as a nexus between **students, industry, and researchers**;
"diversity of perspective breeds innovation"; "we model ourselves off the world changers of
today to make world changers for tomorrow."

## Mepo — the mascot

- **Mepo the platypus**, named **15 July 2025** at the leadership handover when founder Rohan
  Kalanje stepped down as Team Lead. Chosen by team vote; **M-E-P-O = one letter from each
  department** (Marketing, Education, Projects, Operations).
- Why a platypus: the team "represents the transition from the digital ocean to the physical
  landscape — no better animal than the Aus native platypus" (Publications meeting notes,
  21/05/2026). Its oddities mirror MNET's many facets of emerging tech: electrosensing bill,
  chews with gravel, sweats milk, glows under UV.
- Appears in *Platypus Forest* (VR environment, O-Week demo), stickers, and comms. Team
  tradition: "The Platypus" has a Members-DB entry (created 10/07/2025) with role **Team Lead**,
  quote "\*Platypus Noises\*", email mnet@monash.edu.

## Meetings & cadence (how the team runs)

- **MNET Schedule** — shared Google Calendar; every member is auto-invited via their Monash
  email; RSVP Yes/No/Maybe ASAP and leave a note if absent (Welcome Pack).
- **Mega Meets** — quarterly, everyone: review the quarter, plan the next, open feedback.
- **Huddles** — monthly per department: align member/department goals, Notion progress trackers.
- **Workerbees** — 1–2×/week per department/portfolio: hands-on sessions, attendance, blockers.
- **Event planning meetings** — per event, all involved members.
- **Leadership All-Hands** — ad hoc, leads only, for major decisions.
- External: **ITTA** (FIT teams ↔ MSTI coordination hub; MNET/METTA a founding participant,
  Oct–Nov 2023), **MSTI Forums** (each trimester, all Eng+IT teams; forum series runs since
  ≥May 2019), **VARS** and **EmVis/IA Lab** meetings, plus supervisor meetings.
- Discord = day-to-day comms; Notion = meeting notes (Meetings + Lead Meetings DBs).

## Ecosystem & governance (the larger Monash picture)

- **HCC**: MNET's academic home is FIT's **Department of Human-Centred Computing**
  (monash.edu/it/hcc — Monash is top-10 worldwide in HCI by ACM CHI papers). Advisors **Prof Tim
  Dwyer** and **A/Prof Tom Chandler** are both HCC; **Keenan Granland** (MSM Lab Manager,
  Faculty of Engineering) is the Engineering-side staff partner.
- **EmVis**: within HCC, the **Embodied Visualisation group** (led by Tim Dwyer; runs the
  Immersive Analytics Lab) — "bringing data out of computers and into the world around us".
  Joint work: **Globes App** (Apple Vision Pro, with **Stanford University**, David Rumsey Map
  Collection; demoed to the Chancellor + Education Ministers of Australia and India at the
  Monash Innovation Precinct; shown at SXSW Sydney), **Bali AVP** (Balinese temple digital
  heritage with the Indonesian government, Gaussian splatting), **BikeVR**, annual EmVis
  showcase (23/10/2025, ~25 attendees).
- **GIM**: FIT's **Games and Immersive Media** major/minors (3D, game design, mocap/character
  animation, VR/AR — monash.edu/it/gim-showcase). GIM students ≈ a fifth of MNET's IT-major
  members (19.4% in 2025); MNET gives them portfolio projects and showcases (e.g. BLAST 3D at
  Monash College, attended by GIM professors).
- **MSTI**: MNET reports an **End of Year Report each December** (MSTI template: demographics,
  operations, events, social data, finances, next-year funding). Funding: 2024 **$4,000** (100%
  MSTI); 2025 **$6,500 MSTI + $2,150 FIT** (~$8,650 spent); released in tranches (2025: $1,600
  Feb + $2,400 if spent by 01/09). Governance details: [STUDENT-TEAMS.md](STUDENT-TEAMS.md).
- **Growth (EOY data)**: 2024 — 38 members (52→10 S1 rebuild recruitment, 182→20 S2), website
  26k visits after mid-year launch. 2025 — ~70 active (roster ~97; 96+40 apps R1, 59+8 R2),
  43.3% international, Instagram 135→642, website ~41k unique visitors, 20+ stakeholders
  (Microsoft, Telstra, EY, Capgemini, HPE, Blackmagic, 360 Productions, Engineers Australia,
  ANSTO, CrowdStrike, XENON, Macquarie Bank, Databricks, Altair, Ventia, MindFlight7, Meta,
  Moderna…).
