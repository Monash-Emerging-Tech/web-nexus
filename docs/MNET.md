## Identity

- **Full name:** Monash Nexus for Emerging Technologies (MNET)
- **What it is:** A student team under the **Monash Student Team Initiative (MSTI)**, affiliated
  with the Faculty of Information Technology (and increasingly Engineering). Everything MNET
  does falls under the **simulation technologies** umbrella — XR / immersive media, digital
  twins, quantum computing — while staying deliberately broad: robotics and other up-and-coming
  technologies are "not out of bounds" (draft constitution). "Emerging" is read as emerging
  **from the digital realm into the physical** — hence the tagline.
- **Tagline (primary):** **"Enter the Digital Frontier"** · supporting lines: *Access, Upskill,
  Engage* (prospectus) · "Expanding all horizons." · "JOIN US, BUILD THE FUTURE"
- **Home:** The **Digital Makerspace** at Monash Clayton. Team postal address: Building 63,
  25 Exhibition Walk, Clayton VIC 3168.
- **Website:** https://monashemerging.tech · **Repo:** github.com/Monash-Emerging-Tech/web-nexus
- **Reach:** ~8,000+ combined online student following (2025 prospectus figure).

## Mission (canonical copy)

From the 2025 prospectus — the most polished statement of what MNET is; prefer this voice:

> "We are the Monash Nexus for Emerging Technologies. We are a student team focused on exposing
> students to the possibilities offered by XR, Quantum Computing, and more. As a 'nexus', we
> offer passionate and talented students the opportunity to collaborate with research labs, work
> in an industry environment, and get their hands on otherwise inaccessible technology.
> Together, we hope to inspire the next generation of innovators."

Canonical umbrella (Rohan Kalanje, founder — confirmed 2026-07-10): **everything MNET does
falls under simulation technologies.** The "emerging" in the name is the bridge — technologies
emerging from the digital realm into the physical — which is exactly the prospectus's "bringing
the real into the digital and the digital into the real", and why the tagline is **"Enter the
Digital Frontier"**.

Impact framing (prospectus): MNET strives to explore and harness emerging technologies to
reshape **"what can't be" into "what will be"** — "bringing the real into the digital and the
digital into the real". MNET serves as a nexus between **students, industry, and researchers**;
"diversity of perspective breeds innovation"; "we model ourselves off the world changers of
today to make world changers for tomorrow."

Draft-constitution mission: "to act as a meeting point between students, industry professionals
and academics in the field of emerging technology so that each can benefit from the
relationship. Students, forward-thinking yet resource poor, are given access to cutting edge
technology, whilst industry and academia can benefit from the creativity and excitement of our
student members."

## Structure & governance (from the draft constitution + handover)

- **Four departments** — Education, Marketing, Operations, Projects — each led by co-department
  leads, each overseeing **Portfolios** (specialised sub-teams) run by Portfolio Leads.
- **Roles ladder:** Team Lead(s) → Department Leads → Portfolio Leads → Senior Members
  (yearly-refreshed; portfolio leads+) → Members → Probationary Members → Alumni (max 6 months
  post-graduation).
- **Term limits:** team leads serve no more than 2 consecutive years; expected commitment
  15–20 h/week; handover runs over ~4 weeks with a vision meeting; outgoing leads shift to
  redirection/advisory/support roles.
- **Leadership selection:** every opportunity advertised months ahead with an open EOI.
  Portfolio leads chosen by outgoing lead + dept leads; dept leads serve a deputy semester
  before elevation; team leads elected by a vote of portfolio/department/team leads, ties
  resolved by outgoing leads.
- **Code of conduct pillars:** honesty & integrity, accountability, fostering inclusivity,
  academic integrity, collaboration, ethics — plus MSTI Policy Manual compliance.
- **Recruitment:** semesterly — see "Recruitment & talent pipeline" below. (The step-by-step
  process notes previously mirrored here from Notion/Drive have been deliberately dropped:
  that process is being re-documented and refined as of 2026.)
- **Meeting cadence** (Notion "New Meeting Structure"): **Mega Meets** (quarterly, whole team —
  progress review, achievements, next-quarter planning) → **Huddles** (monthly, per department)
  → **Workerbee sessions** (1–2×/week, hands-on task work) → event-planning meetings, plus
  ad-hoc **Leadership All-Hands** (leads only) and external forums (ITTA/MSTI, VARS Monday
  standups, IA Lab meetings).
- A **DRAFT MNET Constitution** exists in the Team Lead Drive folder (its governance/amendment
  sections are unfinished); the repo's `docs/constitution/MNET-Constitution-DRAFT.docx`
  consolidates it with the handover-doc policies.

  application/interview process notes previously copied from the Notion workspace and the Drive
  handover docs have been deliberately removed from these docs — do not republish them. The

  culture points toward.

  skills to those studying art or design" (Monash course page, retrieved 2026-07-10) — which
  matches MNET's mixed technical/creative intake across IT, Engineering, Design, Fine Art,
  Science and double degrees.

## Where MNET sits at Monash (HCC context)

- MNET's academic home ground is the Faculty of IT's **Human-Centred Computing (HCC)
  department** ("Putting people first"). Monash is ranked **#10 in the world in HCI** by ACM
  CHI paper acceptances (HCC page, retrieved 2026-07-10).
- **Embodied Visualisation (EmVis)** — MNET's principal academic partner (Tim Dwyer) — is one
  of HCC's research groups: "exploring new immersive display and interaction technologies to
  aid data analysis and decision making." MNET effectively operates as a student-team
  counterpart to the HCC ecosystem — hands-on simulation-technology projects feeding off and
  into human-centred computing research.
- Adjacent HCC groups are natural collaborators as MNET broadens: the **Emerging Technologies
  Lab** (design, use and futures of new technologies), **Exertion Games Lab** (play, technology
  and the active body), **SensiLab** (creative applications of technology), and Inclusive
  Technologies.
- In FIT's community structure, MNET is a **student team**, not a club: "a self-managed,
  multidisciplinary group under expert mentorship" offering professional experience (FIT "Join
  the community" page). Clubs/societies are the social layer; student teams are the
  professional layer; **MSTI** is the initiative that administers the teams.

## Flagship project story — Globes (the "Stanford" feature)

Semester 1 2024: **Globes**, built for Apple Vision Pro, lets educators present historical
globes and show how Earth changed over millennia, with SharePlay multi-user collaboration.
Initiated by **Prof. Bernhard Jenny** (cartography/geovisualisation); developed by senior
member **Boo Sung Jung** with testing by Rohan Kalanje and Jordan Zdimirovic. Via Prof. Jenny,
the team worked with **Stanford University and the David Rumsey collection** to ship it to the
App Store — **hundreds of thousands of downloads** worldwide. Boo Sung then taught Swift
internally and landed a software-engineering placement. This is the anchor story for the
"Stanford" featured project on the site.
