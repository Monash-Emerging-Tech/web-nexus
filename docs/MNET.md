## Identity

- **Full name:** Monash Nexus for Emerging Technologies (MNET)
- **What it is:** A Monash University student team working on **emerging simulation
  technologies** — VR/AR/XR, digital twins, quantum computing, motion capture, and the web
  frontier (WebXR).
- **Home:** The **Digital Makerspace** at Monash Clayton — a central hub supporting digitally
  focused student teams from the faculties of Engineering and Information Technology.
- **Website:** https://monashemerging.tech
- **Repo:** https://github.com/Monash-Emerging-Tech/web-nexus

## Structure — four departments

From the Notion Team Structure page (verbatim descriptions):

- **Education** — "works with academics and student organisations (such as teams and clubs) in
  pushing forward and educating in the field of emerging technologies. They are also responsible
  for training our team and getting them up to scratch with working with the newest emerging
  technology by developing training modules." Runs workshops (Maya, Unity, Intro XR, Spline,
  Womp, Pictionary, Git), game jams, and school/uni outreach (SPARK, Minaret, Open Day, MacRob).
- **Marketing** — "deals with reaching stakeholders through the creation to share upcoming events
  or milestones achieved through social media content alongside developing merchandise, working
  with 3D printing & other machines." Owns the website, podcast (weekly), email list
  (fortnightly/monthly), socials (LinkedIn, Instagram), O-Week presence.
- **Operations** — "works on the internal infrastructure of the team alongside managing and
  developing relationships with internal and external stakeholders. They are the skeleton of the
  team and keeps us running!" Owns Notion, hosting, guides, sponsors, finance, recruitment,
  asset management.
- **Projects** — "dedicated to driving the core initiatives of the team by overseeing project
  development, execution, and management." Active projects include CampusGuessr, BrainXR,
  Quantum, Peptrac, Platypus Forest, Time Travel, CAIVR, Robodog.

Departments oversee **Sections** (sub-teams), and a **Leadership Team** (team leads, department
heads, senior members) guides direction. Academic advisor: **Dr Tim Dwyer** (Embodied
Visualisation group, Faculty of IT).

## Notion integration reference

The site pulls content from the MNET Notion workspace (`src/lib/notion/`):

- **Portfolios DB** — database id `6c76fb82-07b2-4915-814f-5a57bd4b06b4`, data source
  `a916ca0f-0797-4d7f-8eb0-fde670e6142d`. Key properties: `Project name` (title), `One Liner`,
  `Description`, `Summary`, `Department` (multi: Education/Projects/Marketing/Operations),
  `Project Type` (3D/VR/Digital Twin/Prototyping/Data Visualisation), `Tech Used`, `Status`
  (Planning/In progress/Done/Canceled/Paused), **`Web Status` (Active/Inactive — the
  publish-to-website flag)**, `GitHub Repository`, `Figma Link`, `Canva Link`, `Dates`,
  `Collaborator`, `Priority`, `Portfolio Lead(s)`, `Assignee(s)`.
- **Members DB** — names, roles, quotes, departments, icons, LinkedIn URLs. Cover photos are
  synced locally via `scripts/fetch-notion-covers.js` into `public/img/members/{page-id}.jpg`.
- Env vars (see `sample.env`): `NOTION_API_KEY`, `NOTION_PORTFOLIOS_DB_ID`,
  `NOTION_MEMBERS_DB_ID`. All fetchers fail soft to DUMMY_ data when unset.
- Website project hub in Notion: "Nexus Command Centre" (`2511d893-3ee3-8003-9e6f-cb664aed0bf2`);
  redesign brief: "Web Redesign" (`1e91d893-3ee3-8085-9004-dd6e6df47f53`).
