## What the Monash Student Team Initiative (MSTI) is

- **MSTI** is the Faculty of Engineering–run initiative that endorses, funds, and governs
  Monash's student teams (Clayton campus). Teams are student-led with academic and technical
  staff advisors, operate mostly extra-/super-curricularly, and accept members from **all
  faculties**, undergrad and postgrad.
- The authoritative list of endorsed teams lives at
  https://www.monash.edu/engineering/student-experience/teams-and-clubs. Clubs and societies
  listed separately are *not* subject to team governance.
- Sister teams that appear throughout the shared drive: Monash Motorsport (MMS), Monash Nova
  Rover, HPR (High Powered Rocketry), MCAV (Connected & Autonomous Vehicles), MUAS/UAS
  (Aerospace), MNR, Monash DeepNeuron (MDN — both an Engineering and IT team), MBEST, MPP, MSB,
  BiOM, SWM, and the FIT-side teams (MNET, MAPS, MATT, Monash AIM, DTAM).
- **MNET's place:** MNET is an MSTI team hosted on the **Faculty of IT** side. Its funding is
  provisioned as "IT Faculty Funding" and it coordinates through ITTA (below) — but it follows
  MSTI-wide procedure, reporting, and asset/travel systems like every other team.

## History (from the drive's own records)

- **2019** — the "Student Teams & Clubs Forum" series is already running (slides archived for
  May, July, September 2019) alongside Student Team Advisers' meetings (Nov 2019). This forum
  series continues today as the trimesterly **MSTI Forum**.
- **2020–2021** — forum archives continue; **2021: the Faculty of Engineering takes over primary
  funding of student teams from the individual Engineering departments** (noted in MSTI's own
  reporting templates).
- **October–November 2023** — the **IT Teams Association (ITTA)** forms as the coordination hub
  between MSTI and the FIT-based teams. Founding meeting (11 Oct 2023, with MATT) lists the
  participating teams: Monash AIM, MAPS, Monash DTAM, **METTA (contact: Jackie Hai Ho)** and
  Monash DeepNeuron. ITTA meeting No. 1 followed on 2 Nov 2023; meetings continued through 2024
  (MNET attended No. 4 — Rohan Kalanje, Zoey Liang). ITTA topics: marketing/merch guidelines,
  finance guidelines, recruitment coordination, O-Week/showcases, escalating shared pain points
  to faculty, and cross-team culture.
- **2023–2024** — "New Leaders Inductions" run annually for incoming team leadership.
- **December 2025** — the Faculty Executive approves a **new organisational structure**: facility
  management (Makerspace network, Design & Build Studio) is separated from student-team
  oversight to remove conflicts of interest. The **Director of Student Teams** becomes a
  strategic-oversight role; operational responsibility moves to professional staff.
- **2 July 2026** — **Student Teams Procedure Version 2** takes effect (review due July 2029).
  Enquiries: eng-student.teams@monash.edu.

## Governance under Student Teams Procedure Ver 2 (effective 02/07/2026)

The Procedure applies to all endorsed teams; teams may not write conflicting procedures (or
their own codes of conduct). Team documents (like MNET's constitution) must align with and
reference it.

**Key roles** (post-Dec-2025 structure): Director of Student Teams (strategy, travel approver);
Lead Academic Advisor (advises up to five teams); Academic Advisors (technical rigour +
go/no-go risk approval, sponsor liaison); Group Manager Strategy & Planning (budget allocation);
Manager, Makerspace Network; Senior/Student Experience Coordinators (operations, finance,
travel, sponsorship agreements); Senior/Technical Officers — risk & safety (after-hours
supervision, inductions, testing safety); Team Executive (student leadership); Staff/Student
Travel Leaders. A governance committee reviews teams, budgets, and new-team requests twice a
year against Impact 2030.

**Conduct & culture:** Student Code of Conduct applies (team code adds to it, never replaces);
zero BAC when representing the team; **all team events are alcohol-free** (Faculty Executive,
May 2026); smoke/vape-free campus; grievances route through discussion → Safer Communities /
MSTI staff / Respect advisors; contribution issues use a separate "Individual Team
Contributions" review process (private, staff present, 360° feedback, may result in being asked
to step off the team).

**Recruitment & membership:**
- Only current Monash students (precise definition incl. month-after-completion grace and
  intermission) may be members; non-students/staff may not.
- **From July 2026 every member (new and existing) reapplies at least annually** — no guaranteed
  reselection.
- Written, accessible applications; diverse trained selection panels; consistent core interview
  questions; conflicts of interest declared; recruitment documentation kept for one year.
- Gender targets: in most teams it is appropriate to interview all non-male applicants; some
  teams have a strict 50:50 recruitment policy set by Faculty Executive.
- Probation 3–6 months with formal review; confirmation → resignation/abandonment (4+ weeks
  uncontactable)/dismissal processes all defined, with property returned within a week.
- **Leadership:** EOI circulated to all members; shortlisting on experience/contribution/
  diversity; election by secret ballot/consensus or panel selection; execs formally endorsed by
  Academic Advisors before announcement. Senior non-technical roles (CEO/COO/CFO-equivalents)
  are interviewed by a gender-balanced panel including staff, on empathy, conflict management,
  ethical reasoning, communication, and culture stewardship. MSTI runs annual leadership
  training.

**Learning, credit & recognition:**
- Onboarding within 3 months: Respect at Monash, OHS inductions, local area inductions, safe
  work instructions, evacuation/first-aid orientation, and the team's finance processes.
- Teams must not encourage skipping classes; pause work during SWOTVAC/exams.
- **CPD:** 1 year of membership = 420 hours under Engineers Australia CPD guidelines (pro-rata;
  minimum 6 months = 240 h); statements signed via the Director/Academic Advisors.
- **ENG4802 Professional Practice (Student teams)** — for-credit unit open to Engineering *and
  IT* student-team members since S1 2024 (2h face-time/week, no exam; 30% online modules + 70%
  e-portfolio built on team contributions, cross-team feedback groups). Part of the four-unit
  Professional Practice suite (ENG4801 curriculum-embedded / 4802 teams / 4803 WIL / 4804 co-op).
  NB: USA-travel assessment restrictions (B1/B2 visa credit issue since 2019) — US trips must be
  co-curricular; OS-HELP loans possible for other destinations under full-time-load formulas.

**Finance, assets & logistics:**
- Purchasing/reimbursement through **Coupa**; merchandise sales must run through **shop.monash**
  (no direct collection of funds); official vs unofficial merch rules with Monash-logo
  requirements and MSTI email approval (eng-student.teams@monash.edu).
- **Reftab** is the MSTI/Makerspace booking portal for vehicles, trailers, and camera/marketing
  gear (induction + licence required for vehicles; pre-tow checklist mandatory; sharing
  etiquette codified).
- Venue bookings via Monash Venue Services (2+ weeks notice), New Horizons via its shared
  facilities calendar, Design & Build studios via eng-makerspace@monash.edu.
- Freight/Carnet appendix documents international shipping practice (Victorian Chamber of
  Commerce carnets, battery/dangerous-goods rules) — mostly relevant to the travelling
  Engineering teams, useful reference if MNET ever ships hardware abroad.

**Travel:** annual mid-year EOI → assessment by a panel (Deputy Dean Strategic Initiatives,
Faculty GM, Director of Student Teams, Group Manager) → endorsement by the Student Teams
Governance Committee → traveller vetting (current student, no misconduct/APC, **WAM ≥ 60 from
2027**) → staff-led planning (Reho travel provider, Dean sign-off; Monash Abroad registration
for international) → monitored trips with 1:20 staff and 1:5 student-leader ratios.

**Reporting & funding cycle:** teams submit an **End of Year Report** each December on the MSTI
template (demographics, operations, events, social media, finances, conclusions, next-year
funding request). Approved funding arrives in tranches (e.g. MNET 2025: $1,600 in Feb, $2,400
released only if all funds spent by 1 Sep). MNET received $4,000 (2024, 100% MSTI) and
$6,500 MSTI + $2,150 FIT (2025).

## How this matters for the website

- "About us" can truthfully anchor MNET in this ecosystem: an MSTI-endorsed, FIT-hosted team,
  governed by the Student Teams Procedure, coordinating through ITTA, reporting annually to
  MSTI, funded by FIT/MSTI, advised by HCC academics (EmVis), and collaborating across the
  Engineering teams network.
- Recruitment pages should reflect Procedure requirements (eligibility, annual reapplication
  from July 2026, probation, accessible written applications).
- Don't publish: staff contact details, finance codes, the Contact List, or leads-only material.
